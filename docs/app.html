Codebooks for cards 1–3 (held by the clinic)

Each card's 15 punch patterns light 15 different codes. Keys are drawn from the 14 patterns that punch at least one valve and leave at least one open.

How a reply is judged
VerifiedMatches the code for today's card and key.
Wrong valvesA real code for today's card, but for a different punch pattern.
Wrong cardA real code for today's key on one of the patient's other cards.
IncompleteSome of the expected glyphs, in order: too little sample, wrong-pH liquid partly, or read too early.
Not a codeNo card and key produce it: a misread, or a guess.

Three tries per day. A key left unanswered when the next day's key goes out is recorded as missed.
